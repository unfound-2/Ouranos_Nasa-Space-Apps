Data and the online assistant

Names and onboarding answers are stored in this browser tab’s session. Hosted watch pairing and readings use Supabase. Selecting Ask or a review question sends the question and displayed measurements to Groq when the online assistant is configured. The local fallback uses bundled guide content. AI responses can be wrong and do not establish a diagnosis.
