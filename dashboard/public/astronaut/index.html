We help astronauts who aren’t doctors understand what’s happening in their bodies and know what actions to take, even when medical support from Earth is delayed.

What is Ouranos?

A watch, a custom shroud and chain band, and a clearer way to understand your health.

The 3D preview is unavailable. Explore the watch’s features alongside it.

What do the features do?

Ouranos brings watch readings into one health dashboard, so you can follow changes, review what stands out and find the relevant NASA guidance.

The shroud

Our custom 3D-printed housing fits around the Apple Watch SE and gives the assembly its shape.

The chain band and back plate

The linked band forms the strap around the wrist. The back plate completes the shroud assembly.

The watch and your readings

The Apple Watch supplies the health readings it supports. Pair it with Ouranos to see trends and check the readings behind a flag.

Guidance when you need it

Review changes in context, ask about your readings and open a linked NASA source. The dashboard supports a medical review rather than making a diagnosis.
