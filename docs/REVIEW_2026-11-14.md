# Independent review, 14 November 2026

After the first working version, three fresh AI reviewers checked the branch, each from one angle and read-only, with a reproduction for every finding:

- **Honesty:** what the running dashboard and the docs actually say.
- **Watch contract:** whether a Swift developer can implement it from `docs/WATCH_INTEGRATION.md` and `contracts/v1` alone, tested against a live server.
- **Server and shared code:** correctness and security.

**Result:** 37 findings. Three were duplicates, which leaves 34 distinct issues. 33 are fixed and 1 is kept on purpose (#22). The fix commit (19f7b27) says "35 were confirmed and fixed"; the counts here are the accurate ones.

## Known limits not covered by a fix

- **Shared device token:** every watch uses one token. A device that holds it can name another watch's `deviceId` when reading the outbox. Delivery reports and acknowledgements are refused when the reporting watch is bound to a different crew member.
- **Untested on hardware:** direct watch-to-laptop HTTP on watchOS and `GG_LAN=1` have not been tested with a real watch.
- **Power-cut durability:** macOS `fsync` does not force the drive's own cache, so writes are best effort if power is lost.
